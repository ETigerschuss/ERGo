### Method 1: Save from Attachment (Easiest)

1. **Right-click** on the ERGo splash image in your message
2. **Select "Save Image As..."**
3. **Navigate to**: `c:\Users\serbe\Desktop\hirnkastl\ERGo!\ERGo!\assets\`
4. **Save as**: `ergo_splash.png`
5. **Done!** Refresh your browser to see it
